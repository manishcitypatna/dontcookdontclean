## What Families in Rajendra Nagar Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Not Discussing Timing Flexibility Upfront
Given how many households here have irregular schedules, assuming a helper can simply "figure out" the timing without discussing it first is a common source of friction. Agree on fixed slots or acceptable flexibility before the arrangement starts.

### Skipping Verification Because "They've Worked in the Area for Years"
Longevity in the neighbourhood isn't the same as a documented background check. Ask for ID and a reference call regardless of how long someone has worked locally.

### Not Planning for Leave During Festival Travel Season
With the terminal nearby, festival travel season affects both families and helpers. Discuss leave expectations around major holidays well in advance rather than assuming availability.
