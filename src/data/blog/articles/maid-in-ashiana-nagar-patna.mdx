## What New Residents in Ashiana Nagar Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Relying on Building Staff Who Don't Actually Know the Candidate
In newer buildings, security or maintenance staff are often new hires themselves and may pass along a name without really knowing the person's history. Treat any referral as a starting point, not a verification.

### Not Budgeting Time for a Proper Trial
New residents in a hurry to settle in sometimes skip the trial period entirely. Even a short 2 to 3 day trial can save a much longer headache later.

### Assuming Babysitting Experience Without Checking
Because Ashiana Nagar attracts a lot of younger families, babysitting requests are common — but general housekeeping experience isn't the same as childcare experience. Ask specifically and get a reference where possible.
