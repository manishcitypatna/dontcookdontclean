## What Families on Fraser Road Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Treating "Everyone Knows Them" as Verification
Long-standing local familiarity isn't the same as a documented ID check or reference call. Do both, regardless of how well-known a candidate is in the area.

### Not Clarifying Duties When Home and Business Are Close Together
If your residence sits above or near a family business, be clear about whether domestic duties overlap with business-related tasks. Undefined overlap is a common source of disputes later.

### Skipping a Trial Period Out of Habit
In areas with deep-rooted informal hiring, trial periods are often skipped because "that's not how it's done here." A short trial costs little and protects both sides regardless of local custom.
