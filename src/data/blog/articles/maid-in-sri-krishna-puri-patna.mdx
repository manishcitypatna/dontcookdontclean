## What Families in SK Puri Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Choosing Based on Availability Alone
With so many options nearby, it's tempting to go with whoever's available soonest. Availability says nothing about whether ID and references have actually been checked — slow down enough to verify before committing.

### Assuming a WhatsApp Group Recommendation Is Equivalent to a Reference
A name shared in a residents' WhatsApp group is a lead, not a reference. Call the previous employer directly rather than relying on a forwarded message.

### Not Clarifying Discretion Expectations Upfront
Established households often have specific expectations around privacy and discretion that go unstated until something goes wrong. If this matters to you, say so clearly during hiring.
