## What Families in Danapur Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Not Starting Documentation Early Enough
If your housing authority requires advance ID submission for domestic staff, leaving this until the last minute delays your helper's start date. Begin the paperwork as soon as you've selected a candidate.

### Assuming All Verification Requirements Are the Same Across Danapur
Requirements can vary between different housing sections within the cantonment. Confirm with your specific authority rather than assuming a neighbour's process applies to you.

### Not Planning for Postings and Transfers
Defence and service families relocate more often than average. If you expect a transfer within the next year, discuss this openly with a prospective long-term helper so expectations are aligned from the start.
