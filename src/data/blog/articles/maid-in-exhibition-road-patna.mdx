## What Working Professionals Near Exhibition Road Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Requesting "Flexible Hours" Without Defining What That Means
"Flexible" means different things to different people. Give an actual range of times you might need help, so the candidate can honestly say whether it works for them.

### Not Discussing Access When You're Not Home
If cleaning happens while you're at work, agree in advance on how the helper gets in and what's expected while you're away. Leaving this vague creates avoidable friction.

### Skipping Verification Because Timing Was the Hard Part to Solve
Once you've found someone whose schedule finally fits, it's tempting to skip the ID check and reference call to lock it in. Don't — verification takes the same day regardless of how hard the schedule match was.
