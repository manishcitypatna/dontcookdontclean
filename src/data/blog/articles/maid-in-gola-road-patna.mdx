## What New Residents in Gola Road Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Hiring Under Time Pressure Right After Moving In
The rush to settle into a new home often means skipping proper verification. Give yourself at least a few days to check ID and call a reference, even if it means a short delay.

### Assuming Every Neighbour Has the Same Standards
In a fast-growing area, neighbours are often just as new as you are. A recommendation from someone who moved in the same month you did carries less weight than it might in an older, more settled colony.

### Not Confirming Whether a Society Requires Staff Registration
Some newer societies have started requiring domestic staff registration for security. Check with your management committee before your helper's first day.
