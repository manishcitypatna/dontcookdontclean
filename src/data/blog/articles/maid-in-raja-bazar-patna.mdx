## What Families in Raja Bazar Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Not Specifying Household Type
Whether you're a standalone family home or a smaller shared household affects what a candidate should expect. Be clear about this from the first conversation.

### Assuming Informal Referrals Are Sufficiently Vetted
As in much of central Patna, referrals here often come through long-standing local networks. Familiarity isn't the same as a documented ID check or reference call — do both regardless of how the referral came about.

### Not Agreeing on Access for Shared or PG-Adjacent Households
If your household involves shared access with other tenants or units, clarify with your helper exactly which spaces and schedules apply to avoid confusion later.
