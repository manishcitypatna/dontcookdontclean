## What Families in New Patliputra Colony Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Prioritising Speed Over Fit
When you're hiring for the long term, rushing the process to fill a gap quickly often backfires. Take the extra day or two to properly check references — it pays off over months, not just the first week.

### Not Discussing Long-Term Expectations Upfront
If you're hoping for a helper who stays a year or more, say so during hiring. Helpers who know the expectation is long-term are more likely to commit to it too.

### Skipping the Trial Period Because "It Feels Like a Good Fit"
Even a strong first impression benefits from a short trial period. It's a low-cost way to confirm before committing to a longer arrangement.
