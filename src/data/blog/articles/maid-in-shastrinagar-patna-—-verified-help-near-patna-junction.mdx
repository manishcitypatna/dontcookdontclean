## What Families in Shastrinagar Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Assuming a Helper Can Adjust to Shifting Timing Without Discussion
Flexibility has to be agreed on, not assumed. If your schedule genuinely varies week to week, say so during hiring and confirm the helper is comfortable with that before starting.

### Not Building In a Backup Plan for Missed Slots
Given how many households here rely on tight timing, a single missed day can throw off a whole week. Have a backup plan — whether that's a second part-time helper or a clear understanding with your primary one.

### Skipping Verification Because Timing Pressure Feels More Urgent
When you need help fast because of a schedule crunch, it's tempting to skip ID checks and references. Don't — a rushed, unverified hire causes more disruption than a short delay to verify properly.
