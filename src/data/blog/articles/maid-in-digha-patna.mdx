## What Residents in Digha Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Assuming Building Staff Vet Every Referral
In newer complexes, security and maintenance staff are often new hires themselves, so a name passed along by them isn't necessarily well-vetted. Treat it as a lead, not a guarantee.

### Rushing the Hire Because of Limited Local Options
With fewer established local networks, the temptation is to hire the first available option. Take the extra day to check ID and call a reference — it's worth the wait.

### Not Planning for River-Adjacent Access Issues
During monsoon season, some parts of Digha closer to the river can see access disruptions. If this affects your area, discuss contingency plans with your helper in advance.
