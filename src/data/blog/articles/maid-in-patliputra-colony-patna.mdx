## Why Hiring in Patliputra Colony Tends to Be More Deliberate

Because homes here are larger and often independent bungalows rather than flats in a shared building, there's less of the informal "maid works three flats in this building" network that's common in denser colonies. Families in Patliputra Colony more often hire directly for a single household, full-time or live-in, which raises the stakes on getting the match right the first time.

That also means fewer built-in checks — no building watchman vetting who comes and goes, no neighbours who already know the helper. Verification has to be done deliberately rather than picked up informally.

## What to Check Before Hiring in Patliputra Colony

### Don't Skip Verification Just Because There's No Middleman
Without a building watchman or a chain of neighbours who already know the helper, the responsibility for verification sits entirely with you. Always ask for a government-issued photo ID and call at least one previous employer directly.

### Be Specific About Live-In Arrangements
Independent houses often have more space to accommodate live-in help than flats do, which makes live-in arrangements more common here. If you're going this route, agree in advance on living space, off-hours, and weekly or monthly leave — informal arrangements tend to drift without this being written down early.

### Confirm Childcare Experience Separately From General Housekeeping
If you need help for a child specifically, don't assume general housekeeping experience covers it. Ask directly about prior childcare experience and, where possible, get a reference from a family they cared for before.
