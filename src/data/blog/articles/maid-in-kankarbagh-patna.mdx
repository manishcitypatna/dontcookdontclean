## Why Hiring in Kankarbagh Feels Different From Other Patna Localities

Because Kankarbagh is so large — stretching from the main road through numerous internal lanes and residential blocks — most hiring here still happens through word of mouth: a neighbour's recommendation, a society watchman, or a maid who already works two flats down. This works often enough that families keep relying on it, but it also means verification is usually skipped entirely, since the referral itself is treated as the verification.

With many joint and multi-generational families in the area, requirements also tend to be more involved than a single task — cooking, elder care, and general housekeeping are frequently bundled into one full-time role.

## What Families in Kankarbagh Commonly Get Wrong When Hiring

### Treating a Neighbour's Referral as Full Verification
A recommendation from a neighbour or watchman is a reasonable starting point, but it isn't the same as an ID check or a reference call. Ask for a copy of a government photo ID and speak to at least one previous employer directly, even when the referral feels trustworthy.

### Underestimating What a "Full-Time" Role Actually Covers
Because so many Kankarbagh households combine cooking, cleaning, and elder or child care into one full-time role, it's easy to under-scope the job description at hiring time and end up renegotiating later. Write down exactly what's included before the helper starts.

### Not Agreeing on Leave and Emergency Cover in Advance
With larger households, an unplanned absence is felt immediately. Agree on a monthly leave count and what happens if the helper is unavailable on short notice, before you need the answer.

## What to Verify Before Hiring in Kankarbagh

1. **Government-issued photo ID** — get a copy regardless of how the helper was referred
2. **At least one previous employer reference**, ideally from a similar full-time or live-in role
3. **A clear, written scope of work** — especially important when cooking, cleaning, and care duties are combined
4. **Leave policy and emergency backup**, agreed before the start date
5. **A short trial period** — 2 to 3 days is enough to confirm fit before committing monthly
